Tsukiji's famous wholesale fish auction relocated to Toyosu Market in 2018 — Tsukiji today is the outer market
area only, with food stalls and retail shops but not the wholesale auction that made it internationally famous.
Worth knowing before booking a hotel specifically for "the fish market" experience.

## What's actually still at Tsukiji

The outer market's food stalls, small restaurants, and retail shops remain and are genuinely worth visiting for
an early breakfast or casual food-focused morning. If you specifically want the wholesale tuna auction
experience, that's now at Toyosu, a separate location connected by train.

## Who staying near Tsukiji still makes sense for

- **Early risers who want to eat breakfast at the outer market stalls** without a long commute beforehand.
- **Travelers prioritizing a quieter, more local-feeling central location** over staying directly in Ginza or
  Shinjuku's busier tourist cores — Tsukiji sits conveniently between several major areas without being as
  saturated with tourists itself.
- **Anyone also planning a Toyosu Market visit**, since the two areas are a straightforward, short train
  connection apart.

## What to check before booking

- **Distance to the nearest station** — the area has decent but not dense train coverage; check walking
  distance from your specific hotel to Tsukiji Station or Higashi-Ginza.
- **Whether you actually want the outer market atmosphere or the Toyosu auction experience** — booking near
  Tsukiji for a wholesale market experience that's no longer there is a common first-timer mix-up.

## Bottom line

Staying near Tsukiji still makes sense for the outer market's food stalls and a quieter central location, but
don't book here expecting to see the famous tuna auction — that experience is now at Toyosu, a separate trip.
