Namba, and specifically the Dotonbori canal area, is where most first-time visitors end up basing themselves
in Osaka — it puts you within walking distance of the city's best-known food scene.

## Why this area works

Namba Station is a major subway hub, so you're well connected to the rest of the city, and you can walk to
Dotonbori's food stalls and Kuromon Ichiba Market without needing transit at all.

## What to expect

This is a lively, sometimes loud part of the city, especially at night. If you want easy access to the food
scene but prefer a quieter place to sleep, look slightly outside the immediate canal-front blocks — you'll
still be a short walk from everything.

## Alternative: Umeda

Umeda is Osaka's other major hub, a bit more business-district in feel, and has slightly better direct
connections to the airport train lines. It trades some walkability to Dotonbori for a quieter base.
