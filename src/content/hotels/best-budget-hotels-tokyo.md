"Budget" in Tokyo hotel terms usually means small rather than low-quality — Japanese business hotel chains are
reliably clean and well-run even at lower price points, which is different from budget hotel experiences in
many other countries.

## Business hotel chains are the sweet spot

Domestic Japanese business hotel chains (rather than international budget brands) tend to offer the best value:
small but efficient rooms, consistent cleanliness standards, and locations clustered around major stations.
Room size is the main trade-off — expect noticeably less space than a similarly-priced hotel elsewhere.

## Where to look for the best budget value

Areas slightly outside the most tourist-dense neighborhoods (a few stops from Shinjuku/Shibuya rather than
directly in them) often have meaningfully lower rates while still being a short, direct train ride from
everything. Tokyo's train network makes "slightly further out but on a direct line" a low-cost trade-off
compared to many other cities.

## Trade-offs worth accepting for budget savings

- **Smaller rooms** — often genuinely tiny by Western standards; check square meterage if this matters to you.
- **Limited or no English-language front desk staff** at the cheapest properties — usually manageable with a
  translation app, but worth knowing in advance.
- **Basic or no breakfast** — many budget business hotels don't include breakfast; factor a nearby convenience
  store or cafe into your budget instead.

## Trade-offs not worth accepting

- **Poor station access** — don't save money by picking a hotel far from any train line; the extra transit
  time/cost usually erases the savings.
- **Shared bathroom facilities you didn't expect** — always confirm whether a listed "budget" room has a private
  bathroom, since this varies more than travelers expect.

## Bottom line

The best budget value in Tokyo usually comes from domestic business hotel chains slightly outside the busiest
tourist areas but still on a direct train line — not from cutting corners on station access or basic room
amenities.
