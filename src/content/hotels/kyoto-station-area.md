For a first visit to Kyoto, the area immediately around Kyoto Station is the easiest place to base yourself.
It's not the most atmospheric neighborhood, but the convenience trade-off is worth it for a first trip.

## Why this area works

Kyoto Station connects directly to Osaka and Nara by train, has luggage lockers and storage for day trips, and
sits at the hub of the city's main bus lines — most of Kyoto's sights are a single bus ride away without
transfers.

## What to look for when booking

Distance from the station matters more than it might seem — even a 10-minute walk difference adds up over
several days of trips back and forth. Hotels directly connected to or facing the station tend to book up
first, so reserve early if you want one, especially during cherry blossom (spring) or autumn foliage season.

## Alternative: Gion or Higashiyama

If atmosphere matters more to you than convenience, staying in Gion or Higashiyama puts you closer to Kyoto's
older streets and temples, at the cost of a longer trip to/from the train station for day trips.
