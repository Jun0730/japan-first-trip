There's no single "best" eSIM for Japan — the right pick depends on trip length, group size, and how much data
you'll actually use. Here's how the main options stack up for different situations.

## Best Japan-specialist option: Sakura Mobile

Sakura Mobile focuses specifically on Japan rather than selling regional or global plans, offers both eSIM/SIM
and pocket WiFi rental, and provides English-language support — a real advantage if activation doesn't go
smoothly right after landing. Worth comparing first if your trip is Japan-only.

## Best for multi-country trips: Airalo

If Japan is one stop on a longer trip, Airalo's single app covering eSIMs for dozens of countries is more
convenient than juggling separate apps or providers per country.

## Best for unpredictable data needs: Ubigi

Ubigi's top-up model suits travelers who aren't sure how much data they'll need and don't want to commit to a
fixed plan size upfront.

## Best for heavy data users: Holafly

If you know you'll be a heavy data user the entire trip — lots of streaming, video calls, or tethering — an
unlimited plan can work out cheaper than a large tiered plan, despite the higher up-front price.

## How to actually decide

1. **Traveling with others who'll stay together most of the day?** Pocket WiFi (Sakura Mobile) usually beats
   buying individual eSIMs.
2. **Japan is your only stop and you know your rough data needs?** A Japan-specialist or Airalo tiered plan.
3. **Genuinely heavy daily data use?** Compare Holafly's unlimited price against a large tiered plan before
   deciding — it isn't automatically cheaper.
4. **Combining Japan with other countries, or unsure of your data needs?** Ubigi's flexibility avoids buying
   several separate plans.

## Bottom line

Don't default to whichever eSIM brand is most familiar — the right choice changes based on trip shape.
Japan-only trips are usually best served by a Japan specialist or a correctly-sized Airalo plan; groups do
better with pocket WiFi; multi-country trips do better with a global brand.
