Airalo and Ubigi both sell Japan eSIM plans on similar underlying networks, so day-to-day connection quality is
rarely the deciding factor. The real differences are in how each app handles buying, topping up, and managing
multiple countries.

## Airalo: simpler for a single, planned trip

Airalo's app is built around browsing and buying a fixed-size data plan ahead of time, with country-specific
and regional plans clearly separated. It's a good fit if you know roughly how much data you'll need and want to
buy once before you fly.

## Ubigi: better if you want to top up mid-trip

Ubigi is generally more geared toward topping up an existing eSIM profile rather than buying a series of
separate fixed plans, which suits travelers who aren't sure how much data they'll need or who are combining
Japan with other countries on one trip using the same eSIM profile.

## Which one should you pick?

- **Japan-only, fixed trip length, know your rough data needs:** Airalo's simpler buy-once model is usually
  easier.
- **Multi-country trip, or unsure how much data you'll actually use:** Ubigi's top-up flexibility avoids buying
  several separate plans across different apps.

## A caveat on pricing

Exact pricing and data tiers change periodically for both providers — always check current tier pricing before
buying rather than relying on older comparisons (including this one), since neither company publishes long-term
fixed pricing.

## Bottom line

For a single, well-planned Japan trip, Airalo is usually the more straightforward choice. If your plans are
flexible or you're combining Japan with other stops, Ubigi's top-up model is worth the slightly less
streamlined buying experience.
