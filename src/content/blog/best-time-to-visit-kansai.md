Kansai has four distinct seasons, and the "best" time depends more on what you're prioritizing — cherry
blossoms, fewer crowds, lower prices — than any single objectively best month.

## Spring (late March–April)

Cherry blossom season is Kansai's most famous and most crowded period. Exact bloom dates shift year to year, so
if seeing sakura is a priority, check forecasts closer to your travel dates rather than booking around a fixed
date. Expect higher hotel prices and to book well ahead.

## Summer (June–August)

June brings the rainy season, and July–August are hot and humid, which can make a lot of walking (which most
Kansai sightseeing involves) uncomfortable. This is also a quieter, cheaper period for hotels compared to spring
and autumn.

## Autumn (November)

Autumn foliage rivals cherry blossom season for popularity, with similarly crowded, similarly beautiful results,
particularly around Kyoto's temples. Like sakura, peak foliage timing shifts year to year.

## Winter (December–February)

The quietest, cheapest season, and still pleasant for sightseeing — Kansai winters are cold but rarely severe.
This is the best trade-off if avoiding crowds matters more to you than seeing blossoms or foliage.

## Our take for first-timers

If your dates are flexible and crowds bother you, aim for winter or early summer (June, before peak humidity).
If cherry blossoms or autumn colors are the whole reason for the trip, accept the crowds and book
accommodation earlier than you think you need to.
