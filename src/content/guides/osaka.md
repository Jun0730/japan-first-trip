If Kyoto is Kansai's cultural heart, Osaka is its stomach. Locals describe the city's food culture with the
phrase "kuidaore" — eat until you drop — and it's a fair description of a night spent working through takoyaki,
okonomiyaki and kushikatsu stalls around Dotonbori.

## How much time to give Osaka

One full day covers the highlights (Dotonbori, Kuromon Market, Osaka Castle); two days lets you add a theme
park visit or a slower food crawl through a second neighborhood like Shinsekai or Namba.

## Getting around

Osaka's subway system is straightforward and covers almost everything a first-time visitor needs. Namba and
Umeda are the two main hub areas — most travelers base themselves near one of these.

## Where to base yourself

Namba/Dotonbori puts you in walking distance of the food scene and nightlife; Umeda is a bit more business-district
and slightly better connected to the airport train lines. See our [Namba & Dotonbori area guide](/where-to-stay/osaka-namba-dotonbori-area/)
for a closer look.

## Suggested pairing

Osaka connects to Kyoto and Nara by direct train in well under an hour each, which is why most Kansai
itineraries treat the three cities as one connected trip rather than separate destinations. Our
[7-day Kansai itinerary](/itineraries/7-day-kansai-first-timer/) allocates two days here.
