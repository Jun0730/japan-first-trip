If Kyoto is the only stop you have time for, this route hits the highlights without feeling rushed.

## Day 1: Fushimi Inari & Higashiyama

Start at [Fushimi Inari Shrine](/things-to-do/fushimi-inari-early-morning-tour/) before the crowds, then spend
the afternoon in Higashiyama around Kiyomizu-dera and the surrounding lanes.

## Day 2: Arashiyama & Gion

Morning in Arashiyama for the bamboo grove and riverside area, afternoon and evening in Gion, Kyoto's historic
geisha district, followed by dinner in the Pontocho alley area.

## Day 3: Nara day trip

Take the train to [Nara](/guides/nara/) for the morning and early afternoon to see the deer park and Todai-ji,
then head back to Kyoto (or onward to Osaka) in the evening.

## Where to stay

[Kyoto Station](/where-to-stay/kyoto-station-area/) is the most convenient base for this itinerary — everything
above is reachable by bus or a short train ride from there.
