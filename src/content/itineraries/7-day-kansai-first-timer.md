This is the itinerary we'd hand a friend visiting Kansai for the first time: enough structure that you're not
wasting time figuring things out, enough slack that you're not sprinting between sights.

## Day 1: Arrive, settle into Kyoto

Fly into Kansai International Airport (KIX) and take the train directly to Kyoto (roughly 75-90 minutes).
Check into your [Kyoto Station area](/where-to-stay/kyoto-station-area/) hotel, and keep the rest of the day
low-key — a walk through the station area and an early dinner is plenty after a long flight.

## Day 2: Fushimi Inari & Higashiyama

Start early at [Fushimi Inari Shrine](/things-to-do/fushimi-inari-early-morning-tour/) — it's open 24/7 and far
quieter before 8am. In the afternoon, walk the preserved streets of Higashiyama around Kiyomizu-dera.

## Day 3: Arashiyama & Nara day trip

Morning in Arashiyama for the bamboo grove, then take the train to Nara for the afternoon to see the park's
deer and Todai-ji. See our [Nara guide](/guides/nara/) for details, or book a
[guided day trip](/things-to-do/arashiyama-nara-day-trip/) that covers both in one go.

## Day 4: Gion & free time

Spend the morning exploring Gion and Kyoto's covered shopping arcades. Use the afternoon as buffer time — Kyoto
rewards unplanned wandering, and by day 4 you'll likely have places you want to revisit.

## Day 5: Move to Osaka

Take the train to Osaka (under an hour) and check into your [Namba or Umeda](/where-to-stay/osaka-namba-dotonbori-area/)
hotel. Spend the evening working through Dotonbori's food stalls.

## Day 6: Osaka Castle & food crawl

Morning at Osaka Castle Park, afternoon at Kuromon Ichiba Market, evening on a
[guided food tour](/things-to-do/osaka-street-food-walking-tour/) if you want context on what you're eating
instead of guessing.

## Day 7: Last-minute shopping & departure

Use the morning for any shopping or sights you missed, then head to Kansai International Airport for your
flight home. Build in at least 3 hours of buffer before an international flight.

## Adjusting this itinerary

Shorter on time? Cut Day 4 and treat Nara as a half-day instead of a full afternoon. Have more time? Add a day
in Kobe or Himeji — both are easy add-ons by train from Osaka.
