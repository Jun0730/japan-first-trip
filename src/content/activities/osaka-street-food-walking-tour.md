Osaka's food scene is dense enough that it's easy to either overspend on tourist-trap stalls or miss the good
ones entirely. A guided food tour solves both problems by taking you to a curated set of stops with a local
guide who can explain what you're eating and why Osaka is obsessed with it.

## What to expect

A typical route moves between Dotonbori's canal-side stalls and Kuromon Ichiba Market, covering staples like
takoyaki (octopus balls), okonomiyaki (savory pancake) and kushikatsu (fried skewers). Group sizes, exact stops
and included tastings vary by operator — check the specific listing before booking, especially if you have
dietary restrictions.

## Good to know

Come hungry — most tours involve several tastings back to back. If you have allergies or dietary restrictions,
message the operator ahead of time rather than mentioning it on the day, since some stalls may not be able to
accommodate substitutions on short notice.
